My Math Notebook — Android Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

My Math Notebook is a math practice app for children and families from Window Box Studios LLC. This policy describes the Android version.

Student notebooks

Student names, avatars, settings, quiz answers, results, and streaks are stored on the device. The app does not require an account, and it does not send notebook contents to Window Box Studios servers. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for student profile data. A parent can delete a student profile and its local history in the app; uninstalling the app removes the local data.

Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads on student home and quiz results screens. Every ad request is configured as child-directed, non-personalized, and restricted to general-audience content. Google and its service providers may process technical information such as IP address, device and app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate their services. We do not use student notebook contents for ad targeting. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob policy information.

Remove Ads purchase

A parent can make a one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play. Google handles payment information, and the app receives purchase status to enable the ad-free version. Window Box Studios does not receive credit card details. The purchase can be restored in the app's Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google Play data handling.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell student notebook contents. We do not operate a server storing those contents. Google processes technical ad and purchase information under its own policies; retention by Google is governed by those policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@windowboxstudios.com.
